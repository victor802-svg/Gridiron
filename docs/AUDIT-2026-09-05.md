is intact (integrity and foreign keys clean, 765 rows, no row edited, no

the operator's to decide. **The single thing to look at first is the
scheduler: re-run `tools/schedule_install.ps1`** — Capture, Recalibrate and both
UFC passes are registered in it and not on the machine, and until that is done
the fights are forecast only when somebody runs them by hand.

## 6. Decisions left to the operator (not fixed, by design)

1. **Re-run the installer.** `tools/schedule_install.ps1` now registers Capture, Recalibrate, Predict-UFC and Final-UFC, none of which the machine holds. Until then the fights are forecast by hand, the weekly re-fit never runs, and the capture pass stamps nothing.
2. **How the tasks run.** They run in a visible console as the logged-on user; two have been killed with a control-C exit. A hidden window, `pythonw`, or a service account are machine decisions.
3. **The league day for college football and UFC.** Both file a game under its UTC date, so a 7:30 PM Pacific kickoff is the next day's slate; the slate key (`games.week`) is derived from the same date and stamped on every prediction. Changing it changes slate identity — a ruling, not a fix. NFL was fixed because nflverse publishes the league's own day.
4. **A fingerprint mechanism.** Today's baseline is recorded above. A per-row hash written at insert (and checked at resolve) is a design; a whole-record hash goes stale with every forecast.
5. **CatchUp on the Health panel.** The one task that has never fired is not a `TaskSpec` and so cannot appear. Making it one is a small design.
6. **Comment-claim scanning.** `docstring_reference_faults` resolves `audit.<name>` in docstrings. Extending it to backticked names in `#` comments found one real fault and 53 false ones in this audit; it needs a rule for which references are claims.
7. **The 42 pre-fix reasoning rows** quote encoded numbers. Re-reasoning them is refused by the answered-once rule; humanising numbers at render time cannot know which number was encoded. They stay as written.
8. **Tap targets by design**: the hero's step dots (20px wide), its arrows (30px) and the sport tabs (40–43px wide, 44 tall). Widening them changes the hero layout.
9. **The browser suite's world** renders no baseball, basketball, college or UFC slate, no Results, no notifications and no task runs — so a plain-words scan there sees a fifth of the app. Seeding those shapes is a fixture design.
10. **The CFB totals method note** rendered 0 visible `card-note` elements on the totals tab at 390 in the cold read while the payload carries it. Look at a college totals card on a phone before ruling.
11. **The reasoning halves of the blocked slates** (baseball 160/161, college 2026-09-04) stay empty for ever under answered-once. A ruling to allow a second forecaster's first answer after the first forecaster's would be a change to that rule.
12. **The header at 700px** still overflows by 80px (FOLLOWUPS).
