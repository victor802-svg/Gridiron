# Brief — GRIDIRON_TODAY (drafted 2026-09-07)

Saved verbatim before any of it was executed.

---

The operator's daily loop, made real: one screen he opens every morning on
desktop, a list he acts on, a tap that records what he took, and a learning
cycle that is visible rather than silent. Nothing here changes how the model
forecasts. It changes what reaches a person and what comes back.

Operator rulings, taken 2026-09-07 and treated as settled:
  * Corrections apply automatically and report afterwards.
  * The daily list is NEVER empty.
  * Desktop is the primary surface.
  * The app records which picks he took.

---

## PHASE T1 — The Today screen

One route, opened daily, desktop-first (the phone layout still has to hold at
390px, per the existing phone pass). It answers one question: what should I be
looking at today?

**Two groups, always both present, never blended.**

1. **CLEARS THE BAR** — recommendations whose edge survives the venue's fee.
   Fair value, the venue's price, edge in cents after fees, the size, and the
   gate status. This group is frequently EMPTY, and when it is, the screen
   says so in a plain sentence rather than reaching down into group two to
   fill itself.
2. **WATCHING** — the rest of the day's shortlist, ranked, **each carrying its
   own edge figure including when that figure is negative**, printed in cents.
   A row reading "−3¢ after fees" is doing its job. The group's heading says
   in one plain line that these do not clear the bar.

**THE RULING THAT MADE THIS NECESSARY, RECORDED HONESTLY.** The operator ruled
that the app must always show him something to look at. It does. The design
answers that ruling without letting the app assert something it has not
measured: a row is shown, and its true edge is shown beside it. **No row in
WATCHING may be described with any word that implies it is worth backing.**
The plain-words scan gains "value", "play", "lock", "best bet", "free money"
and "worth it"; `tools/guards/plant.py` puts one in a WATCHING label to prove
the scan fires. The line between showing and recommending is a scanned one,
not an intention.

**Below the fold, one honest line of arithmetic**, present every day: the
venue's fee on a bet at today's median price, in cents, so the cost of a
no-edge bet is on the same screen as the temptation to make one.

## PHASE T2 — "I took this"

One tap per row. It writes a row to a new append-only table recording the
prediction id, the timestamp, and nothing else.

**WHAT IT RECORDS AND WHAT IT REFUSES TO, and why the distinction is the whole
design.** LAW 5 forbids the operator's wagering ledger inside this repository,
because a model that can see its own profit and loss is one step from fitting
to it. That law stands. This table therefore records **which pick, and when** —
never a stake, never a price paid, never a payout, never a result in money.
`audit.check_taken_is_not_a_ledger` fails by name on a money-shaped column in
that table, and a planting adds one to prove it fires.

**STRUCTURALLY EXCLUDED FROM EVERY MODEL INPUT.** No training query, no
correction fit, no factor, no rung choice and no ranking may read it. The
operator takes perhaps a quarter of the list and takes it for reasons; those
picks are a biased sample of the model's own work, and a model that learned
from them would be learning his habits rather than the sport.
`audit.check_taken_not_in_training` reads the SQL of every training and
correction query and fails by name if the table appears; a planting joins it
into a training query to prove the scan fires.

**WHAT IT IS FOR:** exactly one question, answerable on 21 September — do the
picks the operator selected score better, worse, or the same as the ones he
passed over? Reported per sport and market with its N, behind the same gate as
every other figure, and reported as three curves (taken, not taken, all)
that are never merged.

## PHASE T3 — Make the learning visible

`correction.py` and `drift.py` already do the learning the operator asked for
and do it correctly: Platt scaling on the record's own claims and outcomes,
eligible at fifty settled rows, applied at write time and never retroactively;
drift measured over two snapshots, gated at fifty pairs. Nothing about that
machinery changes. **It is currently invisible, which is why the operator
believed it did not exist.**

Add a "What the record has taught it" panel, one row per category:

- the correction's status — not yet eligible (with how many more rows), or
  fitted, with its slope, its intercept, its N and the date it was fitted;
- what that means in one plain sentence: "claims near 70% have been worth
  about 62%, so they are now published lower" — the number, then the words;
- the drift verdict, or the count and nothing else below its gate;
- the date each was last refitted, so a stale correction is visible as stale.

**A correction is dated and never rewrites a prediction** (LAW 3). The panel
must make that obvious: old predictions keep the number they were written
with, and the correction changes what gets written next.

## PHASE T4 — What the close-out must state

- The count of days since the observation window opened, and how many of them
  had an empty CLEARS THE BAR group. If that fraction is high, it is the
  honest headline about how often a real edge appears, and it belongs in
  FOLLOWUPS rather than being smoothed over.
- Confirmation that no training or correction query reads the taken table,
  proven by the planting rather than asserted.

## Close-out

Standard table: one row per phase, four verdicts, evidence nameable.

## How this brief is read (written at receipt, before any work)

**The four rulings are settled and are not re-argued.** In particular "the
daily list is never empty" is implemented exactly as the brief resolves it: the
list always has rows, and every row carries its own true edge, including a
negative one. The app shows; it does not assert.

**T2's table is the sharpest edge in this brief and it is treated that way.**
It records a prediction id and a time. Two scans stand over it -- one refusing
a money-shaped column, one refusing its appearance in any training or
correction query -- and both are proved by plantings rather than asserted. The
brief's reasoning is the right one: the operator's selections are a biased
sample of the model's own work, and a model that learned from them would learn
his habits rather than the sport.

**T4's empty-group count is reported even when it is embarrassing.** If the bar
is cleared on few days, that fraction is the honest headline about how often a
real edge appears, and it goes into FOLLOWUPS in those words.

**The observation window that began on 2026-09-07 is not interrupted by this
work.** This brief adds a surface and a record; it changes nothing about how
the model forecasts, which is what that window is watching.
