per market; LAW 6 throughout; the operator's markets: MONEYLINE

Save the brief as docs/briefs/2026-09-03-ufc.md first.

 U1 PROBE (read-only, docs/UFC_FEASIBILITY.md): does the ESPN core
    API carry UFC events, fighters, results with METHOD and ROUND
    (needed to resolve rounds and distance), and moneylines / round
    totals? Coverage across the last 20 cards. Historical depth for
    walk-forward (years). A second free source for fighter stats
    (reach, stance, finish rates) — verify it exists and its
    robots/terms; if it is bot-blocked like PrizePicks, the build
    uses ESPN only. Identity: fighter names across sources, measured
    raw and normalised, ambiguities refused (fighters share names
    more than teams do). Cadence: cards per month, fights per card.
    Void rules to write first: cancelled bout, weight-miss
    replacement, no-contest, doctor stoppage between rounds (say
    how the total resolves), and a fight declared before the final
    card is set.
 U2 DATA + RATING: loader for events, bouts, results, fighter
    profiles; an Elo-style fighter rating computed walk-forward from
    results (declared, dated, K measured not guessed — report the
    K that best predicts held-out results across the history and
    state it is a fitted constant). Loud on empty.
 U3 FACTORS, declared with rationales: rating difference; layoff
    days each; short-notice flag (bout announced < 21 days out —
    measure the announcement field first, else absent); age gap;
    reach gap; finish rate rolling; missed-weight flag if the source
    carries it; asked line for the rounds market. Explicit-absent
    everywhere; variance bookkeeping.
 U4 MARKETS: moneyline (fighter A wins), total rounds at the
    market's posted line (declared ladder from the measured line
    distribution; the asked rung nearest the model's expected
    length), goes-the-distance. Own categories, own gates. Market
    comparison straight from the moneyline's implied probability
    (no SD needed — binary); for rounds, the posted line's price
    pair. Walk-forward sanity, labelled. Expect the market to win.
 U5 SURFACES: UFC tab; fights as "A vs B" (no home/away); pick
    sentences via side_named ("Pereira to win", "Under 2.5 rounds",
    "Goes the distance — no"); the plain-words scan; countdown to
    the next card. Live tiles: round-by-round scores are not a
    thing — a live bout shows "in progress · round 2"; final shows
    the verdict chip and the method in words.
 U6 QA: the ticked checklist per market; plantings (a UFC query
    merged with another sport; a rounds total merged with the
    moneyline curve; a rating fitted with a K chosen by hand; a bout
    predicted after its start; a fighter identity matched by
    guess); renders desk and 390px; the first forward card written
    blind with snapshots after; verify.py; /closeout; push.
