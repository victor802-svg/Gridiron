THE CARDS UI
Save as docs/briefs/<date>-cards-ui.md. Approved by the operator from a
rendered mockup (gridiron_cards.html — if the file is in docs/mockup/
use it; if not, build from this spec and say so). Presentation only.

RULINGS:
R1 ONE LAYOUT for every width. The desk (frame + rail) and the compact
   rows are DELETED — code, CSS, and the tests that pinned them — with
   zero allowlist additions, the way calls were withdrawn. Their
   guards that still apply (no truncation, plain words, side_named,
   colour law, breakpoint agreement where a breakpoint remains) are
   re-pointed at the new layout, not dropped.
R2 LESS DENSE. One number per card: the model's chance. The market's
   number and the three-sentence why are one tap away (card expands
   in place). Nothing else on a card but game, pick, chance, time,
   tier.
R3 THE HERO shows the sharpest disagreement for the selected market
   tab by default (sort toggle still switches it to most confident).
   Arrows and dots step through the top five.
R4 MARKET TABS per sport, under the headline: MLB — All · Moneyline ·
   Run line · Totals · Hits · Home runs · Strikeouts; NFL — All ·
   Spread · Moneyline · Total · then each prop stat; NBA and CFB
   likewise; UFC — All · Moneyline · Rounds · Distance. Each tab
   carries its count; zero-count tabs stay visible. The hero follows
   the tab. Tabs come from the sport's declared market list — never a
   hardcoded row (a fifth market must appear without a UI change).
R5 STRONG remains the default tier filter (R2 of GRIDIRON_17); the
   sub-line says how many didn't clear the bar.

PALETTE / TYPE (dark, soft): bg #0F1114, card #171A1F, raised #1D2127,
hairline #262B33, text #F4F5F7, muted #9AA3AF, faint #5F6975; --win
#22D37A only on picks that won and "right" counts; --loss #FF6B6B only
on losses. Type: Manrope (or Inter if unavailable) — self-hosted — 800
for game names, 700 labels, 600 body; NO condensed caps, NO monospace
anywhere in the UI. Radii 22px cards, 28px hero, 999px pills. Motion
vocabulary unchanged (150/200ms, ease-out, opacity/transform/
background/border only).

STRUCTURE (desktop >=900px; single column below):
- Top: wordmark "Gridiron" left; pill nav Picks · Record · Results ·
  Settings right. Below: sport pills with the record ("MLB 85–49",
  "UFC 0").
- H1 "Tonight" (or the slate's day in words); sub-line: "13 strong
  picks · first pitch in 2h 14m · 15 more didn't clear the bar".
- Market tabs (R4).
- HERO: tag ("Sharpest disagreement tonight"), game in full names
  ("Padres at Reds"; UFC "Hooker vs Parnasse"), pick sentence, the
  three-sentence why, tier pill with its earned line, time and venue;
  right side a rounded block with the chance at 72px and beneath it
  "chance Cincinnati wins" and "the market says 41%" small. Arrows +
  dots for the top five.
- "More picks" grid, 3 across: game (22px 800), pick sentence, chance
  (40px 800) with "chance" beneath, time and tier small right. Hover
  reveals "market 46% · tap for the why"; tap expands the card in
  place with the why, the market line, and the what-it-knew line.
  "show all N →" reveals the rest; default shows six.
- Yesterday strip: "Yesterday: 6 right, 3 wrong" (green on the right
  count only) · season record · the next-verdict countdown.
- Footer line: one number per card; market and reasons one tap away;
  green only on wins; sports never summed.

GUARDS: every existing planting that applied to the old picks screen
is re-pointed (two forecasters in one ranking; a resolved row on
Picks; a day key in visible text; ellipsis anywhere; prose built in JS;
a market tab not derived from the declared list; a card showing more
than one number collapsed). Read-cold pass on five cards. Renders at
1440 / 900 / 390 before commit: the hero, the tabs on two sports, an
expanded card, a UFC card, the yesterday strip. Full suite, no skips;
verify.py green; /closeout.
