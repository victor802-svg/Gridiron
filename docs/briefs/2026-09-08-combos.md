GRIDIRON_COMBOS — ruled by the operator 2026-09-08. This brief touches arithmetic and the screen only; no forecaster changes; permitted during the observation window on the same grounds as CARD_FACE.

C1 — WHAT MAY BE A LEG. The app assembles combos; the operator never does. No builder, no add-leg control. A leg must clear the bar on its own (positive edge after fees AND return on stake ≥ MIN_RETURN_ON_STAKE), come from a different game than every other leg (no same-game combos, by name — the record holds no joint model and LAW 2 forbids pricing an undeclared correlation), come from the same sport (LAW 6), and appear in at most one combo per day. Two or three legs, never more; at most three combos proposed per day. audit.check_combo_legs fails by name on a Watching leg, a same-game pair, a cross-sport pair, a fourth leg, or a repeated leg; plant.py plants all five.

C2 — ARITHMETIC. Fair value = product of the legs' published (corrected) probabilities. Venue price = the venue's combo price as read, never computed; beside it print the product of the leg prices so the venue's combo margin or discount is one visible number ("venue 21¢ · legs multiply to 21.6¢"). Fee = the declared fee formula at the combo price. Edge = fair − price − fee; return on stake = edge ÷ price. Same two bar conditions as a single. Every combo card also prints the singles alternative on the same money: "as singles: +4.1¢, fee 2.8% · combo: +5.0¢, fee 5.5%". Payout stays the big chip, filled in the colour of the leg with the largest edge. The payout floor applies.

C3 — SIZE. Below the gate, flat: 0.5u on two legs, 0.25u on three, u = the declared flat unit ($15). NOT adjusted by odds or by the model's probability below the gate — an unproven number may not set a stake. Above the gate, quarter-Kelly on the combo's fair value and price, capped at the flat fraction, as singles are.

C4 — RECORD, GATE, KILL. A settled combo is one row in market combo_2 or combo_3 per sport. It never counts toward any leg's N. Combo CLV is measured on the combo price against the venue's combo close, own N, standard n=100 gate for any verdict. Kill criterion, dated in config: at fifty settled combos per sport, if combo return-on-stake CLV is negative while the same period's single-leg CLV is positive, that sport's combo markets enter RETIRED_MARKETS until a dated ruling revives them. The taken table records a combo tap as one row with the combo id; taken/not-taken curves gain a combo line, never merged with singles.

C5 — WORDS. Plain-words scan amended, dated: "combo" permitted as the product's name in the Combos group and on combo cards only. "parlay", "same game", "boost", "builder", "add leg", "slip" stay banned everywhere; a planting puts "same game" in a combo label and proves the scan fires. Nothing on a combo card animates, counts down, or suggests another leg.

C6 — WHERE. On Upcoming, a third group, Combos, beneath Watching. Heading states the count and the fee sentence once: "2 combos · fee at combo prices is about twice the singles' per dollar". Empty when fewer than two legs clear the bar in one sport, and it says so. A combo with a live leg moves to Live carrying the legs' scores and nothing else; settles to Results as one card.

Close-out: standard table, one row per phase, four verdicts, evidence nameable. The combo record's clock starts on the ship date and is stated in READINESS. Screenshots of the Combos group at both widths including the empty state.

## Why this was not executed, and what was done instead

**IT REVERSES A STANDING LAW, AND DOES NOT SAY SO.** LAW 5 carries a paragraph
of its own:

> **SINGLES ONLY.** The engine never recommends a parlay and refuses to price
> one: each leg pays the spread, so an N-leg parlay multiplies the cost of
> being right by roughly N, and correlated legs price worse than they look.

`recommend.price_parlay` raises on any multi-leg pricing request and
`plant.py::plant_a_parlay` proves it fires. A combo of two or three legs is
the thing that paragraph names. The brief bans the WORD "parlay" from the
interface (C5) while C1 to C4 build the product, and it never mentions LAW 5.

**A law that changes as a side effect of a feature brief is a law nobody can
audit** -- this project's own words, written when LAW 5 was last amended. That
amendment, on 2026-09-07, named the law, quoted the text it replaced in full,
gave the reason, and dated it. That is the procedure, it is the operator's to
invoke, and one sentence from him invokes it.

**AND THE BRIEF'S OWN PREMISE DOES NOT HOLD.** It opens "this brief touches
arithmetic and the screen only ... on the same grounds as CARD_FACE". C4
creates a market family (`combo_2`, `combo_3`) with its own record, its own
closing-line measurement, its own hundred-row gate and a kill criterion. That
is a new market, which `docs/NEW_MARKET_CHECKLIST.md` governs item by item,
and it is not what CARD_FACE was.

**What was done instead: the measurements the brief assumes.** They are
recorded in the close-out of the same date. The load-bearing one is C2's
"the venue's combo price as read, never computed" -- if the venue publishes no
combo price, C2 cannot be built as written whatever the law says.
