=====================================================================
PHASE D2 — the frame (commit "desk 2: frame")
=====================================================================

Per the mockup, at >= 1280px:
- Page does not scroll; the frame does. Frame height = viewport
  minus the bar; overflow scrolls with no visible scrollbar (all
  engines), bottom fade mask as the cue. Sticky controls row at the
  top of the frame (sort toggle, market filter, slate count).
- Tiles: three across, ~124px tall, tier as a 3px left edge (white
  STRONG / grey SOLID / faint LEAN), matchup in condensed caps,
  pick line in short form (team +/-line · time; "Under 55.5 total";
  "Tulsa to win"), market % and gap small in the corner, model % in
  mono large with a two-word label. No truncation anywhere: assert
  no text-overflow ellipsis in the frame.
- No sections. One grid, sorted per the toggle; rank numeral shows
  the position in the current sort.
- Voided rows never render (standing rule); "N below the 70% floor"
  in the count line where props exist.
- Keyboard: arrow keys move selection through tiles; Enter/Space
  selects; focus visible.

=====================================================================
PHASE D3 — the rail (commit "desk 3: rail")
=====================================================================

- SELECTED PICK panel, always populated (first tile by default):
  matchup, subline (time · venue · market), pick sentence, the
  probability rail (model dot solid, market dot hollow, shaded gap),
  model / market / gap in mono, the three-sentence why from
  language.py, the tier line, "How the model works ->". Updates on
  tile click without any frame movement (assert scrollTop is
  unchanged after selection).
- AT A GLANCE panel: kickoff windows as thin bars with counts,
  line coverage per market (spread / total / moneyline), the
  sharpest disagreement of the slate, and the tier status line
  ("all CFB tiers unproven — 0 settled" until otherwise). All
  server-computed, plain words, N on every claim.
- SINCE YOU LAST LOOKED panel: the greeting content moves here on
  the desk (per-sport marker unchanged); notices collapse into one
  line inside it, expandable.
- All three panels are one implementation each with the mobile
  surfaces they replace — no second composer, no second query.

=====================================================================
PHASE D4 — phone unchanged, proven (commit "desk 4: breakpoints")
=====================================================================

Below 1280px the compact rows render exactly as before; below 760px
the phone layout as before. Headless renders at 1440, 1280, 1100,
390 — the desk at the first two, rows at the third, phone at the
last — and a test that each breakpoint shows the intended layout
(frame present / absent). Tap targets >= 44px on phone; no overflow
at any width; the hidden scrollbar does not break wheel, trackpad,
or touch scrolling (drive a scroll in the browser test and assert
scrollTop moved).

=====================================================================
PHASE D5 — verification (commit "desk 5: qa")
=====================================================================

1. Full suite, no skips; new plantings: a rung chosen by rotation;
   a tile with ellipsis; a rail panel building prose outside
   language.py; a frame selection that moves scrollTop.
2. Renders before commit at all four widths, plus the desk with a
   tile mid-list selected after scrolling (rail updated, frame
   still).
3. The D1 measurement and the before/after claim distribution in
   the close-out, plainly framed: the new rungs make STRONG rare;
   fewer confident picks is honesty, not loss.
4. verify.py green; /closeout, verdict slots empty; push.
