STEP 2 — SOURCES AND HONEST CONFIDENCE (a short session; complete
it before anything else after UFC). Save as
docs/briefs/2026-09-03-sources.md.

 S1 CAPTURE WHAT WAS KNOWABLE, WHEN. Three sources are stored
    without a capture time or are backfilled after the fact, which
    is why the timing probe could not measure three sports:
    - injuries (NFL 55,554 rows, no timestamp; NBA ~75 rows; CFB
      none): a scheduled fetch of the injury/inactive feed every
      4h in season, every row stamped captured_utc, never
      overwritten — the report's history becomes data.
    - lineups/probables (MLB 99.4% backfilled): capture live on the
      same cadence from T-6h, stamped; the backfill rows keep a
      "backfilled" mark so they never pose as live captures.
    - weather: store the FORECAST as of prediction time beside the
      later observed reading, so wind fits on what was knowable,
      not on hindsight (the CFB wind coefficient is currently fitted
      on observed and applied to forecasts — a ceiling the close-out
      already named).
    Each with a loud-on-empty rule and a Health row. These make the
    A1 timing probe measurable for every sport by October.
 S2 WHAT IT KNEW. Every prediction row already records which
    factors were present. Surface it as one plain line on the card
    and in the rail: "rested on 7 of 9 factors; the starter wasn't
    announced yet; travel couldn't be measured." Plus the age of
    the newest data behind it ("data as of 2h before first pitch").
    Server-composed in language.py; plain-words scan; a planting
    for a card whose factor count disagrees with its row.
 S3 THE CONFIDENCE LABEL TELLS ITS OWN RECORD. The tier chip already
    carries the earned line once a band clears 20. Below the gate,
    the line reads the honest thing: "STRONG — 8 settled, not yet
    proven"; above it, the earned rate. Add the per-sport largest-
    gap sentence to the rail's tier line so a reader sees the
    model's worst band beside the pick's band.
 S4 THE LLM FORECASTER IS GRADED. Confirm it is writing rows on
    every slate since the key was replaced, that its rows resolve
    and enter its own calibration category, and put its N beside
    the statistical model's on Record. If it is not writing, that
    is a defect, not a footnote.
 S5 PLANTINGS: an injury row without captured_utc; a backfilled
    lineup posing as live; a factor fitted on observed weather where
    a forecast row exists; a "what it knew" line disagreeing with
    the row. Suite, verify.py, /closeout, push.
