THE OPERATOR'S RULINGS:
R1  Record shows PROGRESS TOWARD VERDICTS — how close each gate is,
    not just that it hasn't cleared.
R2  History is BOTH a results calendar and the table.
R3  Settings live IN-APP and are EDITABLE — for operational knobs.
    Model constants and laws are shown, never edited here.
R4  FOUR top-level pages: Picks · Record · History · Settings.
R5  KILL Digest and KILL Versions. Their facts survive; their pages
    do not.
R6  Schedule folds into Settings as the HEALTH section; failure
    notices still travel to the rail and front page. Factors becomes
    "How the model works", a section reachable from Record — one
    card per factor.
R7  LOGIN shows the record and the slate size — never a pick.
    Sessions are 30-day sliding; after login the app opens on Picks.
R8  BRAND: the wordmark as is; the dumbbell mark as favicon and PWA
    icon; no light mode.

PHASE P1 — the record shows how close it is (commit "prog 1: gates")
Every gate in the app becomes visible progress, in one component:
- TIER ROWS: beneath each tier's verdict, a thin progress line to
  the 20-settled verdict gate ("14 of 20"), and once past it, to
  the 100 edge gate. Chrome on hairline — no green (a filling bar
  is not a win).
- A "CLOSEST VERDICT" line at the top of the Record tab, per sport:
  "Closest to a verdict: LEAN — 6 more settled, about 2 slates at
  the current pace." Pace from the last 14 days, LABELLED an
  estimate; below 7 days of history, "pace unknown".
- The other counters get the same treatment: corrections ("fitted
  at 50, applied ~200: 31 of 50"), drift pairs ("0 of 50"), the
  rung window ("read on Sep 14 — 12 days"). One component, plain
  words, N on every line.
- Plantings: a progress line rendering a percentage in place of a
  count; a gate line without its N; green on a progress bar.

PHASE P2 — the season as a shape (commit "prog 2: calendar")
History, per active sport, gains a RESULTS CALENDAR above the table:
- One square per day with a settled result; wins–losses inside in
  small mono ("4–3"). Green-tinted when more right than wrong,
  red-tinted when fewer, neutral when even or single-result.
- Voids as a faint dot count, never wins or losses.
- Click a day: the table filters to that day; selected ring; a
  "clear" control restores the season.
- Weeks as rows, months labelled, today outlined; empty days as
  hairline squares.
- If operator calls exist, a toggle switches between "model" and
  "you (informed)" — never combined.
- The table keeps its sentence rows, chips and filters.
- Plantings: a calendar merging sports; a void counted as a loss; a
  square tinted by anything other than the day's balance.

PHASE P3 — settings, with a fence (commit "prog 3: settings")
OPERATIONAL knobs (task times, quiet hours, notification toggles,
the ntfy topic) are editable in-app. MODEL AND LAW constants (props
floor, ladders, gates, thresholds, factor sets, margin SDs) are
SHOWN read-only with their date and "changing this is a ruling;
edit config with a dated note." A planting that makes any editable
fails by name.
- Operational settings move OUT of .env into an append-only
  settings table with a current view; .env keeps secrets only; the
  topic shown MASKED with a "rotate" action via make_token.
- Sections in plain words: "When it runs", "Quiet hours",
  "Notifications", "Access" (rotate token, rotate topic, sign out
  everywhere, this build), "Health" (per task last-ran / result /
  next-due, MISSED rows, staleness, live-poll counts, last
  notification — one implementation with the rail notices), and
  read-only "Rulings in force".
- A schedule change re-registers the OS task via the installer,
  then RE-READS the OS scheduler and shows what it actually holds;
  failure shown in words.
- Every change authenticated, CSRF-protected (per-form token),
  logged to settings history shown as "recent changes".
- No mockup string is copied into Health — every cell comes from
  task_runs.
- Plantings: an unauthenticated settings write; a model constant
  editable from the page; a schedule change claimed without the OS
  re-read; a settings row updated in place instead of appended.

PHASE P5 — four pages (commit "prog 5: four pages")
- Nav becomes Picks · Record · History · Settings. Digest and
  Versions routes redirect (digest -> Picks; versions -> Record's
  model section) — no dead links.
- DIGEST: route and page go; the greeting data stays; digest day
  history becomes a History filter ("resolved on <date>").
- VERSIONS: page goes; "what changed, when" moves into the model
  section as a short dated timeline (two changes named + "and N
  smaller").
- SCHEDULE -> SETTINGS > HEALTH as in P3.
- FACTORS -> RECORD > "HOW THE MODEL WORKS": one card per factor
  per sport — plain name large, one-line "what it measures",

  "unproven — 8 picks"), code name small beneath (the one
  sanctioned code). A search box filters by plain name. Per-pick
  decomposition stays reachable from the pick's rail panel only.

  four nav entries and that every old route redirects.

  picks tonight") — counts and records only. PLANTING: any
  prediction, side, team-with-line, or probability on the login
  route fails by name.

  everywhere" in Settings > Access.

  attach fails. The footer's build line stays.
