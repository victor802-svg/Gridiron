  are already as ruled; nothing there changes. What is new is that the finding
  has to leave the close-out and reach `METHODOLOGY.md`, which is the document
  a reader consults to know what this project believes about itself. §6 already
  keeps a section called "the worst thing the record says"; that is where it
  goes.

  keep CFB on rungs, and make sure the market is *visible with its caveat*
  rather than quietly dropped. The last part needs checking rather than
  assuming — "shown as such, not hidden" is a claim about the interface.
- **Ruling 3 confirms the status quo and makes it a decision.** Game markets
  have no floor today by omission; after this they have none by ruling, and
  the difference is that a later session cannot add one without overturning a
  dated decision.
