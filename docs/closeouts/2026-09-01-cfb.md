## 2. Deviations from the brief, stated up front

1. **R-A cannot be implemented as written; LAW 1 forbids it.** The brief asks
   for questions "only for lined games". Choosing which questions to ask by
   what the market has priced makes the market an input to the prediction
   path, and the closure scan forbids the adapter from naming a market table
   at all. **Questions are formed blind for every game and the R-A number is
   REPORTED instead** — measured coverage makes the gap small (spread and
   total 100%, moneyline 73%). Flagged for a ruling, not worked around.
2. **Player props are not built.** Zero prop rows on completed and upcoming
   games alike; a CFB event carries exactly one odds provider whose
   `propBets` endpoint 404s. The build shrank to the three team markets the
   evidence supports, which is what the brief said to do if a market died.
3. **`predict:cfb` runs daily, not weekly.** A college week is three slates —
   Saturday 60 games, Sunday 16, Friday 8 — and a weekly task would ask one
   question of three different cards.
4. **"Unattended contract" is undefined in this repository.** The only
   occurrence of the word anywhere is a K0–K4 line about the scheduler. Read
   as "work through all six phases without stopping for answers"; recorded in
   the brief file rather than guessed at silently.

## 4. Vacuous passes found, and the class fix for each

1. **A guard crying wolf about correct data.** `test_side_arithmetic` kept its
   own copy of `YES_SIDE`; `language.YES_SIDE` gained a `total` entry and the
   copy did not, so every college totals card read as though its stated side
   were the no side — 40-odd cards reported as disagreeing with arithmetic
   that was exactly right. *Class fix:* the test imports the one map. Two
   implementations of one map is a §3 red flag, and this is the cost — not a
   wrong answer, but a guard nobody will believe next time.
2. **The suite passed in halves and failed as a whole.** I had been splitting
   it to fit a timeout; the arithmetic test reads the live record, and the
   halves ran before the college slate existed. *Class fix:* the whole suite
   is run in one process before any claim that it is green.
3. **`SNAKE_CASE` had been matching nothing** — a `\b` corrupted into a literal
   backspace, so the plain-words law's shape-detecting half was dead and only
   its explicit name list worked. Fifth instance of that corruption. *Class
   fix (shipped as the ruling):* every scanner declares a known-positive and
   known-negative fixture, checked at import.
4. **A test hardcoding three sports, four times over** — a tab count, a
   freshness-row count, a staleness set, a catch-up task set. *Class fix:* all
   four derive from `config.SPORTS`.
