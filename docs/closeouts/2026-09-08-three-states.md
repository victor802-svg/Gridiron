## What the work found

**The two-control-rows rule was anchored on a landmark this brief deleted.**
`audit.picks_control_rows` measured "above the hero", and with the hero gone
it could find no landmark and failed by saying so — which is the right
failure. The rule keeps its number and takes a new landmark, the first card;
the two rows above it are the market chips and the state tabs, so Picks
carries fewer controls above its first card than when the rule was written.

**A live card assembled from the priced entries came out empty.**
`recommend.for_predictions` calls `refuse_in_game` and drops a question whose
game has started — correctly, because nothing may be sized in-game — so a
card built from its output did not lose its prices when the first pitch was
thrown, it vanished. Live reads the cards and asks each one what state its
game is in.

**The accent colour was painting the wrong club.** "Houston covers +1.5" came
out in Philadelphia's red, because the accent followed the prediction's
subject and every number on the card follows the question. That is the
wrong-side defect for the fourth time in three days, in paint; the accent now
uses the same answer the prices use.

**A `const` went out with a function that had grown around it.** Excising the
View menu took `const viewChoice = new Map()` with it, and the page threw
`viewChoice is not defined` on every render. Found by opening the page, which
is the only place it could have been found: the suite was green.

**A stale script in the browser's own cache cost twenty minutes.** The served
file had the fix and the page kept running the old one, through a `no-cache`
header that had fixed exactly this on the 7th. A new tab loaded it correctly.
Recorded because the next person to change the renderer will lose the same
twenty minutes.

**Two versions of one old defect, caught by the tests written for it.**
Switching sports left fifteen cards of the previous sport in the groups,
because `renderToday` ran only in the branch that had cards. Switching to a
market with no picks left the previous market's cards there, because the chips
filtered the slate beneath the groups rather than the groups. Both were found
by tests written in September, when an empty branch left a hero standing.

**Picks can no longer show the reasoning pass.** The View menu was the only
control that chose a forecaster. The choice still exists, the API still serves
it and the day strip still names it, but nothing on the screen sets it. That
is a consequence of a removal the brief asked for by name; it is in
`FOLLOWUPS.md` rather than hidden here, and whether Picks needs a control at
all is the operator's call.

## Rulings taken in the operator's absence

1. **The full slate stays under the groups.** The brief lists what Upcoming
   IS and what is removed; the grid is in neither list, and it is the only
   place a non-shortlisted question appears.
2. **The state tabs replaced three segmented controls rather than joining
   them.** Had they been added beside the sort, the tier and the View menu,
   the page would have carried four rows of controls above its first card and
   the rule would have been right to fail.
3. **The sport tag colours live in the stylesheet, not in `config.py`.** The
   contrast tool reads `:root`; a hex in Python as well would be a second copy
   to disagree with. The club colours stay out of CSS for the opposite reason:
   there are 281 of them and they are measured data.
4. **The "Yours" badge was re-measured, not chosen.** The first amber set
   white at 3.47:1, under AA for text that size. The one on the page clears
   5.36:1 and is in the tool's list.
5. **Live shows a taken pick even when the ranker left it off the
   shortlist.** That ranking is about which questions were worth asking before
   the game; this tab is about the game he has money on.

| 4 | Confirm the unit | **DEFECT FOUND AND FIXED** | The unit was right at $15; the denominator was not. See below. |

**The rationale, for the record.** A switch on the card face lets the operator
pick whichever forecaster flatters each pick, which would make `picks_taken` a
record of his cherry-picking rather than of one forecaster's work. A Settings
default attributes every taken row to one forecaster for a stretch of days,
and the settings table is append-only, so the stretch is dated at both ends.

**The cap was being exceeded by half again.** `unit_dollars` read 15 and was
right. `BANKROLL_UNITS` read 100 — one unit is one per cent of the ring-fenced
money — and a $15 unit against a $1,000 bankroll is one and a half per cent.
At 100 units the declared 2% ceiling resolved to 2.00 units, and two units at
$15 is $30: **three per cent of the money, in the one constant that exists to
stop that.** It had been so since the constant was declared on 2026-09-07 with
the note "awaiting the operator's own number".

**Nothing was staked on it.** No recommendation has ever been above its
market's gate, so every size on the record is the flat unit: 8 rows, all
`flat`, all 1.0 units. The ceiling binds only a gated market and there are
none.

Set to 66.667 units, dated, with this ruling as the reason. One unit is 1.50%
and the 2% cap pays $20. No amount is written into `config.py`: the
denominator is a ratio, and the money stays in the settings row the operator
typed, so LAW 5's ledger prohibition is untouched.
