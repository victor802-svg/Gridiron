## 2. Bugs I introduced, and how they were caught

1. **The U1 harness ran two browsers against one snapshot server** and the
   latency faked a dozen "the click did not take" findings. Caught by
   re-driving one at a time in the probes; every one vanished. Lesson in
   FOLLOWUPS.
2. **The race test was vacuous in its first form**: it delayed requests not
   yet sent, and those went out with the new sport. Caught by running it on
   the unfixed code and watching it pass. Rewritten to click the second sport
   only once the slow request is in flight; it then failed before the fix.
3. **A commit landed with its test red** (`0eb3002`): `pytest | tail` masked
   the exit code, the thing my own notes warn about. Caught by reading the
   output; fixed in `291e89b`; the later chains keep `$?`.
4. **I overwrote `tests/test_controls.py`**, a tracked file, with new tests.
   Caught by the Write tool reporting "updated" rather than "created"; restored
   from HEAD before any commit; the new file is `tests/test_every_control.py`.
5. **The health humaniser said "the slate of Today"**: it reused the relative
   day words. Caught by its own test; a task record wants the absolute day.
6. **The contrast test's premise was wrong**: the tokens are 5.5:1 and the 4.36
   came from an ancestor's opacity. Caught by the test failing on the premise;
   the test now asserts the improvement.
7. **The tap-target change overflowed the phone by 5 px**: five 44 px dots and
   two 44 px arrows in a 312 px row. Caught by the existing overflow test
   (`test_the_page_does_not_scroll_sideways_on_a_phone`); the dots are 32 wide
   and the sport-tab gaps give up the pixels.
8. **The U1 digest labelled finding 24 "[OK]"** — my note() treated a hero
   still showing on a zero tab as words present. Caught while writing the
   two-tabs test, which found cards from another market on every tab.
