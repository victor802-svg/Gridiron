## 2. Where I did not follow a ruling, and why

**R1 says "Friday 09:00". I installed 09:00 DAILY.**

A college week is three slates, and the data is not close: Thu 6, Fri 8,
**Sat 60**, Sun 16. A Friday-only task forecasts the eight and misses the
seventy-six, and cannot pick them up later — `predict` refuses a slate that
has already started and records it MISSED, which is correct and would happen
every Saturday of the season. `tasks.TASKS["predict:cfb"]` also declares a
24-hour cadence, so a weekly trigger would leave Health reporting the task
silent six days in seven and the failure channel firing on it.

`docs/closeouts/2026-09-01-cfb.md` already recorded the same conclusion: "a
weekly task would ask one question of three different cards."

**The hour is yours and unchanged.** To make it Friday-only, change `-Daily` to
`-Weekly -DaysOfWeek Friday` in `tools/schedule_install.ps1`; the line is
commented with this reasoning.

**R3 says replace 4.71 with 4.534. I did not, and the ruling rests on a mistake
of mine.**

The feasibility probe reported that 4.71 "did not reproduce". It does not
reproduce because **I compared two different statistics**: 4.71 is a RESIDUAL,
`SD(actual margin − market spread)` over 2,110 lined finals, and 4.534 is the
RAW `SD(margin)` over 9,373 finals with no line subtracted. They were never
going to agree.

4.71 also cannot be re-derived from this database at all — `measure_margin_sd.py`
deduplicates across every database holding lines for a sport, and this one
holds **67** MLB games with both a score and a line.

Putting 4.534 into that slot would understate how far results land from the
line, in the direction that makes the market look beatable — the shape of the
NBA error `MarginSD`'s own docstring exists to describe. So **4.71 stands,
annotated**, and the raw figures a build actually needs are recorded dated in
`config.MLB_SCORE_DISTRIBUTION`. The probe now carries the correction.

## 3. What fell out of doing the work

- **Three MLB rows where ESPN contradicts itself.** `mlb_823010` is priced home
  −101, away −120 and its `favorite` flag says home. Marked `contradicted`,
  signs untouched, excluded from the verified check. A build must refuse them.
- **Keying the standing forecast on `prop_type` split ten questions** that are
  the same one: the subject already carries the stat, and the earlier run left
  `prop_type` NULL where the later set it. Dropping it made calibration agree
  with Picks at 78.
- **The standing subquery has to mirror the factor-set filter.** Without it,
  asking for fs1 matched the fs2 row's id, failed the outer filter and returned
  **nothing at all** — a silent empty curve.
- **The 2026-08-29 double slate was the legitimate exception**: fs1 at 05:55,
  fs2 at 07:34. The new write-time guard would not have stopped it and should
  not have. What it stops is the same model answering twice, and it now refuses
  out loud — the old behaviour wrote nothing and said nothing, which is how
  three days passed.
