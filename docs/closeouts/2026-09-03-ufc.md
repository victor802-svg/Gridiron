## 2. The declaration, verified

Six tables carry a sport CHECK. All were rebuilt, and the record was checked
rather than assumed:

```
games        21,527 -> 21,527      predictions     519 -> 519
session_seen    408 ->    408      factors          83 ->  83
model_fits       44 ->     44      factor_scores     0 ->   0
```

The `predictions` fingerprint — a SHA-256 over every field LAW 3 protects,
across all 519 rows — was **byte-identical before and after**. The same check
ran again for the `market_type` widening that admits `rounds` and `distance`.

**The rebuild broke half way, and that is now a recoverable state.** `games` is
referenced by seven other tables, so renaming it aside repointed their foreign
keys and the DROP failed *after* the copy: the transaction rolled back and left
an empty `games` beside 21,527 rows in `games_narrow`. Nothing was lost and
nothing recovered it either. The widening now runs with foreign keys off — as
the verified `predictions` path always has — and **resumes a half-finished
rebuild it finds on start**.

`WIDEN_ON_SIGHT` named one table and now names every table that carries the
CHECK. Its own docstring said the old defect "has now bitten three times in one
build"; a list extended by hand each time a sport is added is a list that will
be short again.

## 3. U3 — what is declared, and what is refused

at all**: short-notice needs an announcement date and missed-weight needs a
weigh-in result, and the source carries neither. A factor that can never be
measured is checklist item 2's broken instrument — not a weak signal, nothing

and says what would bring it back.

## 4. The rounds ladder and the void rules

Measured over 2,482 bouts: **2.5 for a three-round bout** (reaches the final
round 55.5% of the time, n=2,398) and **4.5 for a five** (50.4%, n=240). Not
1.5, though a book posted it — over 1.5 on a three-round bout is 72.1% here,
and a rung right three times in four measures the schedule rather than the
model.

**19 stored bouts claim FOUR scheduled rounds**, which is not a UFC bout
length. They are refused rather than believed, and get no rounds question.

Void rules written before the first prediction, per the probe: a no contest
voids **every** market; a draw voids the **moneyline only** — the bout still
went its length, and that is a fact about time rather than about who won; a
doctor's stoppage needs no special case and the docstring says so out loud.

## 5. What is PARTIAL, and what needs you

### 5.1 The market comparison is not wired

U4 asks for the comparison to come from the moneyline's implied probability.
**The probe proved it is there** — two providers per bout, explicit favourite
flags, and both an opening and a closing price — but no UFC fetcher exists in
the market module, so no snapshot is written and no UFC card shows a market
number. The forecasts stand on their own, which is what "no line" already means
everywhere else.

This is the largest remaining piece of UFC and it is a session's work: a fetcher
inside the market quarantine, snapshots after the prediction row exists, and the
LAW 1 closure planting that goes with them.

### 5.2 The scheduled tasks are not installed

`predict:ufc` and `final:ufc` are registered and named, and the OS holds
neither. Installing tasks is an authorised act and this session did not have one
for these. `tools/schedule_install.ps1` does not yet offer them either.

## 6. Bugs of mine, and how each was caught

| what | how |
|---|---|
| `build_features` returned a bare vector where every other adapter returns a pair | the first real predict run |
| **`YES_SIDE` never learned the two new markets**, so all 14 rounds and distance cards showed one side and decomposed the other | **the side-arithmetic guard**, naming all 14 by id |
| Fights rendered "A @ B" and "covers +4.5" | **by looking at the rendered slate** |
| The tile line said "yes dist" | **by looking**, at 390px |
| `mirror_bouts` defined and never called | **the orphan scan** |
| A sport with no loader stub | **the refresh stub rule** |

**One of those was not UFC's.** A contribution of exactly `0.0000` was being
described as "pulling the other way" — two fighters with identical reach. The
supporters/opposers split classifies on a strict sign, so a zero belonged to
neither list and the fallback labelled it as opposition it never offered. Fixed
in shared code: **a factor that moved the answer by nothing is not a reason.**
Any sport could have hit it; UFC hit it because identical reach is common.
